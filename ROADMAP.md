# Course Roadmap

## Phase I — Antenna theory
1. Frequency, wavelength, electrical length
2. Current, voltage, and radiation
3. Complex impedance and resonance
4. Reflection coefficient and VSWR
5. Radiation patterns
6. Efficiency and RF power budget
7. Bandwidth and geometry

**Gate:** trainee can predict basic dipole behavior and explain impedance/pattern plots.

## Phase II — Modeling
8. NEC/xnec2c construction
9. Numerical convergence
10. Sensitivity and parameter sweeps
11. Verification and validation

**Gate:** trainee can build a model from scratch and provide evidence that the numerical result is stable.

## Phase III — HF/NVIS
12. Ground and image effects
13. HF propagation versus antenna behavior
14. Low horizontal dipole
15. Inverted-V
16. Crossed/sloping AS-style reference
17. Mutual coupling
18. Feed/balance/common mode

**Gate:** trainee can explain why height, ground, geometry, and coupling alter an HF/NVIS antenna.

## Phase IV — Capstone
19. Performance metrics
20. 5–35 MHz characterization
21. Soil study
22. Deployment robustness
23. Candidate comparison
24. Final design and credibility review

**Final deliverable:** defensible 5–35 MHz HF/NVIS antenna design study plus prototype measurement plan.
