# Lesson 24 — Final Design and Validation Plan

**Phase:** 5–35 MHz NVIS Capstone

## Objective
Produce an engineering package another engineer can reproduce, challenge, prototype, and measure.

## Final report
1. Requirements and provisional assumptions
2. Final geometry and deployment drawing
3. Solver/version and model configuration
4. Numerical verification and warnings
5. R(f), X(f), VSWR and accepted-power results
6. Efficiency/realized gain where supported
7. G(f,elevation) heatmap
8. Zenith/high-angle metrics, P_high, P_low and high/low ratio
9. High-angle azimuth uniformity
10. Current distributions explaining important transitions
11. Soil sensitivity
12. Deployment robustness
13. Controlled comparison with reference architectures
14. Known omissions and limitations
15. Prototype measurement plan
16. Model credibility statement

## Prototype validation
Plan measurements that could falsify or support the model, including physical dimensions, feed-point/VNA sweeps, common-mode current where practical, selected field-strength/pattern proxies, ground characterization, and repeat deployments.

## Credibility classification
For major conclusions state whether they are:
- numerically verified
- supported by independent physics
- measurement validated
- still unvalidated
- strongly environment-dependent

## Final defense
Answer:

> Why should we trust this model for this engineering decision?
