# Lesson 21 — Soil and Environmental Sensitivity

**Phase:** 5–35 MHz NVIS Capstone | **Target:** 2 hr plus compute

## Objective
Quantify how uncertainty in ground electrical properties changes design conclusions.

Never use only "poor," "average," or "good" ground in final data. Record numerical conductivity sigma (S/m) and relative permittivity epsilon_r.

## Study
Choose at least three representative numerical ground cases spanning environments of interest.

Record R+jX, VSWR, efficiency, zenith gain, P_high, P_low, high/low ratio, and peak elevation.

Start with representative low/mid/high frequencies before expanding the matrix.

## Height interaction
For at least one architecture evaluate:

```
ground properties × antenna height
```

because ground sensitivity can depend on h/lambda.

## Questions
- Which metric is most soil-sensitive?
- Do comparative conclusions change with soil?
- Does matching move more than radiation behavior?
- Is there a modeled ground condition where a requirement fails?
- What ground property should be measured during field validation?

## Deliverable
Soil-sensitivity matrix, plots, assumptions, and a statement identifying which conclusions remain robust.
