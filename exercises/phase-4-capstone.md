# Phase IV Capstone — 5–35 MHz HF/NVIS Design Study

## Mission
Design and characterize a rapidly deployable HF antenna emphasizing useful high-elevation radiation where propagation permits. Use the crossed/sloping AS-style model as a reference, not an assumed optimum.

## Required band
5–35 MHz.

## Evidence
Electrical: R(f), X(f), VSWR, accepted-power fraction, radiation efficiency and realized gain where supported.

Radiation: zenith gain, 60/70/80-degree gain, peak elevation, high-elevation azimuth variation, P_high, P_low, high/low ratio, and frequency/elevation heatmap.

Use current distributions to explain major resonances and pattern transitions.

Evaluate multiple numerical soil-property cases, at least one height/soil interaction, and realistic deployment tolerances.

Provide representative numerical convergence evidence and document warnings.

Compare the final candidate with the low horizontal dipole, inverted-V, and crossed/sloping reference.

## Prototype plan
Specify what will be measured after construction and how measurement will be compared with simulation.

## Final question
The capstone is complete only when the engineer can answer:

> What evidence would cause you to reject your own design conclusion?
