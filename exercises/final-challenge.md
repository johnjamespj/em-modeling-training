# Final Challenge — Unknown Antenna Investigation

## Mission
You are given an antenna model with incomplete documentation. Your job is not merely to run it. Your job is to determine whether its predictions deserve engineering trust.

## Required workflow
1. Inspect geometry and solver settings.
2. State the likely physical behavior before running it.
3. Identify the operating-frequency region.
4. Examine impedance and resonance.
5. Examine current distribution.
6. Examine radiation pattern.
7. Perform at least one parameter sensitivity test.
8. Perform at least one numerical convergence test.
9. Identify important omitted real-world effects.
10. State what conclusions the model can and cannot support.

## Report
Keep the report to roughly two pages plus figures.

Include:
- objective
- assumptions
- prediction
- model description
- numerical settings
- results
- verification evidence
- limitations
- engineering conclusion

## Evaluation
The strongest submission is not the one with the prettiest plots. It is the one that makes the clearest distinction between **solver output, physical interpretation, and justified engineering conclusions**.
